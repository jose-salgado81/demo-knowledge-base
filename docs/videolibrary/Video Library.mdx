# Video Library

This section showcases the video capabilities of this Knowledge Base (KB). Ideally, explainer videos should be embedded directly within the context of their corresponding KB articles.

##  YouTube videos

Example of a YouTube video embedded in the article (position and size can be adjusted):

<div style={{ position: 'relative', paddingBottom: '56.25%', height: 0, overflow: 'hidden', borderRadius: '8px' }}>
  <iframe 
    src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/l1E2oLydIRM?si=k36wNcjU-99rpnJK" 
    title="YouTube video player" 
    frameBorder="0" 
    allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" 
    referrerPolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" 
    allowFullScreen
    style={{ position: 'absolute', top: 0, left: 0, width: '100%', height: '100%' }}>
  </iframe>
</div>

## Video Files

All modern browsers have an integrated media player, allowing visitors to play MP4, WebM, and other video files directly within the article. Unlike YouTube videos, there are no advertisements or Calls-to-Action (CTAs) prompting users to leave the KB.

It is also possible to preset the media player controls. In the following example, the mp4 video is preset to mute, displayed at 50% width, and centered on the page.

  
<div style={{ display: 'flex', justifyContent: 'center', margin: '20px 0' }}>
  <video controls width="50%" muted>
    <source src={require('./img/local.mp4').default} type="video/mp4" />
    Your browser does not support the video tag.
  </video>
</div>



## Interactive Cards

Sometimes, during a video, you may want to point viewers to ancillary videos or articles. For example, if a video covers Topic B, you might want to recommend watching a video about Topic A first.

In the following example, a custom card is configured to appear at the 9-second mark and disappear at the 18-second mark. Every aspect of the card's appearance and timing can be customized. In this example, rather than linking to another video, this card directs you to a specific section within a Knowledge Base article.


import InteractiveVideo from '@site/src/components/InteractiveVideo';

<InteractiveVideo 
  startTime={9}
  endTime={18}
  title="📌 Recommended watch"
  text="If you haven't already watched the Overview of the Brainstorm workspace, watch it now."
  linkUrl="/docs/invitationdialogue/Login%20Requirements#step-by-step-guide"
  linkText="Watch Overview Brainstorm workspace →"
/>
