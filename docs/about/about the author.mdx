# About the author

This demo knowledge base is built and maintained independently.

My name is José Manuel Salgado Arriaga, and I am based in Mexico. I hold a bachelor’s degree in oceanography from [FCM UABC](http://fcm.ens.uabc.mx/) and a master’s degree in education from [IIDE UABC](https://iide.ens.uabc.mx/).

For a few years, I worked as a creative writer, producing articles such as [these on Career Karma](https://careerkarma.com/blog/author/jose/). In 2022, at a client's request, I pivoted into software documentation, helping Publicis Groupe write version 2 of the user guide for its internal project management software (rOar PPM). Realizing the importance of video in illustrating software functionality, I also became an advanced, certified user of [Camtasia](https://www.techsmith.com/camtasia/).

After completing self-guided training and certifications in data science, I joined AnyTrack as a technical writer, client success specialist, and knowledge base manager. From 2023 to 2025, I authored or coauthored many of the articles found in the [AnyTrack Knowledge Base](https://readme.anytrack.io/docs).

Since 2025, I have worked as an independent freelance contractor for [XLeap](https://www.xleap.net/), helping produce a series of software explainer videos. In late 2026, I developed this demo knowledge base site for portfolio purposes, incorporating industry best practices and standards while relying exclusively on free, open-source technologies that ensure full data sovereignty.
